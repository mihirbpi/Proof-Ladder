There are two ways to fill a box.

**Way one.** Put things in, one at a time. This ball. This bear. This block. Slow!

**Way two.** Say a **rule**. *All the round things.* Whoosh — the box fills itself.

That is a **rule box**.

Here comes a marble. Round? Yes. It hops in.

Here comes a book. Round? No. It stays out.

You never had to touch anything. The rule did all the work.

A **rule box** is best when there are too many things to pick up.

<TryIt>
Say a rule out loud, like *all the soft things.* Now look around and say three things that hop in and two that stay out.
</TryIt>
