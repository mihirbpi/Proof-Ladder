Two hoops on the floor. They cross in the middle.

Left hoop: soft toys. Right hoop: blue toys.

<Figure alt="Two overlapping circles. The left one holds soft toys, the right one holds blue toys, and the middle overlap holds toys that are both soft and blue.">
  <VennTwo labelS="soft" labelT="blue" shade={["both"]} />
</Figure>

Here is a blue bunny. Soft? Yes. Blue? Yes. It goes in the middle — the **overlap**.

Here is a blue truck. Blue but hard. Right hoop only.

Here is a brown bear. Soft but not blue. Left hoop only.

The **overlap** is the fussy part. Only things that pass *both* tests get to sit there.

And everything in either hoop, all together? That is the whole pile.

<TryIt>
Make two hoops with string. Pick two rules. Now sort five toys and see how many land in the overlap.
