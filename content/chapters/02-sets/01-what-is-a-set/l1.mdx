Here is the toy **box**. Some things go in. Some things stay out.

A truck? In.

A teddy bear? In.

A shoe? Out! A shoe is not a toy.

Now, the **box** needs a rule everybody agrees on.

*All the red things.* That is a good rule. Everyone can point at red.

*All the nice things.* That is a bad rule! Is a spoon nice? Sam says yes. Pip says no. Now nobody knows what is in the box.

A good **box** rule never makes people argue.

<TryIt>
Make a box on the floor with your hands. Say a good rule. Then put three things in and leave three things out.
</TryIt>
