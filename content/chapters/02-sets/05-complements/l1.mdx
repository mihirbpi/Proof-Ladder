The toy box is full of toys. Now let's find everything that is **outside** it.

Go! Point at things. A lamp — outside. A shoe — outside. A spoon — outside.

<ThingBox boxes={[{ label: "the toy box", items: [{kind: "ball"}, {kind: "block"}, {kind: "star"}] }]}
  outside={[{kind: "sock"}, {kind: "book"}]} outsideLabel="outside it" />

But wait. How far do we go?

If we mean *this room*, then the **outside** is the lamp, the rug, and the chair.

If we mean *the whole world*, then the outside also has boats. And whales. And the moon!

So before you say **outside**, you have to say outside of *where*.

Same box. Different room. Different outside.

<TryIt>
Put three toys in a pile. Now say what is outside the pile — but only in this room. Now say what would be outside if we counted the yard too.
</TryIt>
