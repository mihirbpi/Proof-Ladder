Here is the big box. All the blocks live in it.

Here is a little box. Only the red blocks live in that one.

Is the little box **inside** the big box?

Let's check! Take any one red block. Just grab one. Now go look in the big box.

Found it! It is in there.

Take another red block. Look again. Found it!

Every red block is also a block. So yes — the little box is **inside** the big box.

Now try backwards. Take a blue block from the big box. Is it in the little red box?

No! So the big box is **not** inside the little one.
