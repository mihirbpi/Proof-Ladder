Two boxes. One is red, one is blue. Do they hold the **same stuff**?

You have to check *two* things. Two! Not one.

**Check one.** Look in the red box. Take out each thing. Is it in the blue box too? Yes? Yes? Yes? Good.

**Check two.** Now look in the blue box. Take out each thing. Is it in the red box too?

Uh oh. A rubber duck! And there is no duck in the red box.

So they do not hold the **same stuff** after all. One check passed. The other did not.

You need both. Always both.
