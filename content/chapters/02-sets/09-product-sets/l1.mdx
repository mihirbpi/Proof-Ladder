Sam has 2 shirts. One red, one green.

Sam has 3 hats. A sun hat, a woolly hat, and a party hat.

How many different **outfits** can Sam wear?

Let's count them out loud.

Red shirt, sun hat. Red shirt, woolly hat. Red shirt, party hat. That is 3.

Green shirt, sun hat. Green shirt, woolly hat. Green shirt, party hat. That is 3 more.

3 and 3 makes 6. Six **outfits**!

Every outfit is one shirt **and** one hat. You pick one from here and one from there.
