Two hoops on the rug. Toys everywhere.

Now find everything that is **outside both** hoops.

<Figure alt="Two overlapping circles inside a dashed box, with only the region of the box outside both circles shaded.">
  <VennTwo labelS="soft" labelT="blue" shade={["outside"]} universe="the rug" />
</Figure>

Look! It is the gray part around the edges.

Now say it a different way. What is out there?

Things that are outside the first hoop — **and also** outside the second hoop.

Both outsides at once!

So *outside both hoops* and *outside this one and outside that one* are the very same thing.

<TryIt>
Make two hoops. Put toys in and around them. Now point to everything outside both. Say it out loud: "not in this one, and not in that one."
</TryIt>
