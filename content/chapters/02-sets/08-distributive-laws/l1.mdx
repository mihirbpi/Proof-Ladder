Three hoops now! Hoop $S$, hoop $T$, and hoop $R$.

Here is a job. Find the toys that are in $S$ **and** also in either $T$ or $R$.

You could do it all at once. Squint hard. Tricky!

Or you could **share it out**. Do it in two little steps.

Step one: which toys are in $S$ and $T$? Point at them.

Step two: which toys are in $S$ and $R$? Point at them too.

Now put those two answers together. Done!

Same toys. Much easier.

<TryIt>
Lay out three hoops or three paper plates. Put toys around. Now do the two little steps and see if you get the same pile as doing it all at once.
</TryIt>
