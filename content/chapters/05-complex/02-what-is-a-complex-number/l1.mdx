Stand up. We are going to make a number by walking.

Take $3$ steps **across**. Good.

Now turn a quarter turn to the left. Take $4$ steps **up**.

Stop! Look where you are standing. *That spot* is the number.

We write it like this: $3 + 4i$.

<Figure alt="A grid with a dot marked three squares across and four squares up from the middle." caption="three across, four up — that spot is $3 + 4i$.">
  <ComplexPlane points={[{re: 3, im: 4, label: "3 + 4i"}]} range={5} />
</Figure>

The $3$ says how far across. The $4$ says how far up. The little $i$ is the turn.

Now try a different one. Take $2$ across, and $0$ up. No turning at all!

That is just plain old $2$. So ordinary numbers are these too — they are the ones that never turn.

<TryIt>
Walk 2 steps across, turn, and walk 1 step up. Say your number out loud: "two plus one i!"
</TryIt>
