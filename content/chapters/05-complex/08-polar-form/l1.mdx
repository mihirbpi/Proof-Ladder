There is a toy hidden in the room. I will tell you where it is.

**Way one:** "Go 4 steps forward, then 3 steps to the left."

That works! Across, then up.

**Way two:** "Turn to face the window. Now walk 5 steps."

That works too! **Which way** to face, then how far to go.

Same toy. Same spot. Two completely different ways of saying it.

Sailors and pilots use way two. They say "north-east, ten miles."

You have been using way one all chapter. Now you know there is another.

<TryIt>
Hide something. Give a friend directions using across-and-up. Then hide it again and give directions using turn-and-walk. Which is easier to follow?
