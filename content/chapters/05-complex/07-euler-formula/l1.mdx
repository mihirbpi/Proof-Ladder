Stand up. Face the window.

Now make a **quarter turn** to the left. You are facing a wall.

Another quarter turn. Now you are facing away from the window.

Another one. Facing the other wall.

One more! Where are you facing?

The window again! You are home.

<Turns turns={[1, 1, 1, 1]} labels={["the window", "a wall", "away", "other wall", "the window!"]} caption="all the way round, back home" />

Four quarter turns and you are exactly where you started. Every single time.

That is what the number $i$ does. Multiply by $i$ and you turn a quarter turn.

Do it four times and nothing has changed at all.
