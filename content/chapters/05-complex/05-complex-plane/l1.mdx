Here is a big grid. Put your finger in the middle, where the two lines cross.

Now walk your finger **across** — $3$ squares to the right.

Now walk it **up** — $2$ squares.

Stop. That is **the spot** for the number $3 + 2i$.

<Figure alt="A grid with horizontal and vertical axes crossing in the middle, and a dot marked three squares right and two squares up from the crossing point.">
  <ComplexPlane points={[{re: 3, im: 2, label: "3 + 2i"}]} range={4} />
</Figure>

Every complex number gets its very own spot. No two numbers share.

Try another one. $1 + 4i$: one across, four up.

Now try $2 + 0i$. Two across, nothing up. You stay on the flat line!

That is where all the ordinary numbers live. Right along the middle.
