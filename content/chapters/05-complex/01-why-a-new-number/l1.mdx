Take a number. Now **fold it onto itself** — make that many rows, with that many in each row.

$2$ folded onto itself is $4$. Up high!

$3$ folded onto itself is $9$. Even higher!

Now try a below-zero number. Take $-2$.

$-2$ folded onto itself is... $4$. Up high again!

Wait. It went *up*? But we started below zero!

Yes. Below-zero times below-zero always comes out above. Every time.

And $0$ folded onto itself is $0$.

So look. Everything lands at zero or above. **Nothing** ever lands below.

There is a whole empty place down there where nothing can reach.

Long ago, that bothered somebody very much. So they built a new number to go there.
