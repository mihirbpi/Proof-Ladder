Stand up. Face the door.

Now turn a **quarter turn** — the same amount as the corner of a square.

Turn that much again.

Look where you are facing! The **other way**. Away from the door.

Two quarter turns took you all the way round to backwards.

## Can a number turn you?

Numbers tell you *how many*. Three apples. Seven blocks.

Say "three" out loud. Did you turn? No.

Counting numbers count. They do not turn you.

## So people made one

They made a brand new kind of number, and turning is its whole job.

Its name is $i$.

One $i$ turns you a quarter turn. Two of them turn you backwards.

That is the big idea. The rest of this chapter is all about it.
