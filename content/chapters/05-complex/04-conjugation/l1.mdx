Draw a line straight across the page. That line is a **mirror**.

Now put a dot above the line. Two squares up.

Where is its mirror dot? Two squares *below* the line, straight down.

<Figure alt="A grid with a dot two squares above the across-line and a matching dot two squares below it, the same distance across." caption="the dot and its mirror dot — across stays, up becomes down.">
  <ComplexPlane points={[{re: 3, im: 2, label: "the dot"}, {re: 3, im: -2, label: "its mirror"}]} range={4} />
</Figure>

Across stays the same. Up becomes down. That is the whole rule.

Now do the mirror **again** to your new dot. Where does it go?

Back home! Right where you started. Two flips and you are back.

Now try a dot sitting exactly **on** the line. Flip it.

It does not move at all! It was already on the mirror.

Those are the ordinary numbers — the ones that live on the line and never budge.

<TryIt>
Fold a paper in half. Poke a hole above the fold. Open it — there is your mirror dot. Now poke one right on the fold and see what happens.
</TryIt>
