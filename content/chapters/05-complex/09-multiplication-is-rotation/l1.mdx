Stand up and face the door.

Make a quarter turn to the left. Stop.

Now make **another** quarter turn to the left.

How much have you turned altogether?

Two quarters. That is a half! You are facing away from the door.

Now try different sizes. Make a quarter turn, then a half turn.

A quarter and a half is three quarters. You are facing the last wall.

**Turns add up.** Always. Do one turn, then another, and you just add them.

That is the secret of joining complex numbers together. Join them and the turns add.
