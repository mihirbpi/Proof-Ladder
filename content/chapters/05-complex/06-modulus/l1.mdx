Put your finger in the middle of the grid. That is home.

Now walk to the spot for $3 + 4i$. Three across, four up.

Here is a new question. **How far** are you from home?

Not how far you walked! You walked $3$ and then $4$, which is $7$ steps.

But you did not walk in a straight line. You turned a corner.

Take a piece of string. Stretch it from home straight to your spot. Now measure the string.

It is shorter than $7$. Straight lines are always shorter than corners.

That string length is the **how far**. Every spot has one.
