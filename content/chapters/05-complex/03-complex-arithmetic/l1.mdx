Stand at the start. Here are two walks.

**Walk one:** $2$ across, $1$ up.

**Walk two:** $1$ across, $3$ up.

Now do walk one. **Then** do walk two, starting from wherever you ended.

Where are you?

You went $2$ across and then $1$ more across. That is $3$ across.

You went $1$ up and then $3$ more up. That is $4$ up.

So you are at $3$ across, $4$ up!

Adding two walks is easy. Add the across bits. Add the up bits. Done.
