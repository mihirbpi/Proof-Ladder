Look at this picture of a farm. A dog. A cow. A pig. A horse. A duck.

<Farm />

Now Sam says a big telling:

> "**Every** animal in this picture has four legs!"

Is Sam right? Look hard.

Dog — four legs. Cow — four legs. Pig — four legs. Horse — four legs.

Duck — **two legs!**

<Farm odd={4} />

Gotcha! You found **one bad apple**.

Sam's telling is knocked down. And you only needed one duck to do it. You did not have to check the rest.

<ThingRow items={[{kind:"apple", color:"red"},{kind:"apple", color:"red"},{kind:"apple", color:"green"},{kind:"apple", color:"red"}]}
  ring={2} caption="one is enough to knock the telling down" />

**One bad apple** is enough. Every single time.

<TryIt>
Look around the room. Say a big *every* telling out loud — say, "every book on this shelf is red." Now go find the one bad apple.
</TryIt>
