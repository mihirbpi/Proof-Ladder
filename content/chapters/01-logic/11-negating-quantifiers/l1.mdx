Look at this picture of a farm. A dog. A cow. A pig. A horse. A duck.

Now Sam makes a big claim:

> "**Every** animal in this picture has four legs!"

Is Sam right? Look hard.

Dog — four legs. Cow — four legs. Pig — four legs. Horse — four legs.

Duck — **two legs!**

Gotcha! You found **one bad apple**.

Sam's claim is knocked down. And you only needed one duck to do it. You did not have to check the rest.

**One bad apple** is enough. Every single time.

<TryIt>
Look around the room. Make a big *every* claim out loud — say, "every book on this shelf is red." Now go find the one bad apple.
</TryIt>
