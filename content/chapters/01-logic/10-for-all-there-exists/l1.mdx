Here is a box of blocks. Two people say two very different tellings.

Sam says: *"**Every** block in this box is red."*

Pip says: *"**There is** a red block in this box."*

<ThingBox boxes={[{ label: "the box of blocks",
  items: [{kind: "block", color: "red"}, {kind: "block", color: "blue"}, {kind: "block", color: "red"}] }]}
  ring={{box: 0, index: 1}} />

Those are not the same! Sam is wrong — look at the ringed block. Pip is right.

To check Sam, you have to look at **every** block. All of them. Even the one at the bottom. It is slow work.

To check Pip, you just dig until you find one red block. Found it! Done. Easy!

**Every** means all of them. **There is one** means at least one.

One is a big job. One is a small job. Now you know which is which.

<TryIt>
Grab a pile of small toys. Say an *every* telling about them. Then say a *there is one* telling. Then check them both and see which took longer.
</TryIt>
