Grandma says:

> "You get dessert exactly when you eat your peas."

That is a sneaky sentence. It is really *two* promises.

**Promise one:** If you eat your peas, you get dessert. Yum!

**Promise two:** If you get dessert, you ate your peas. So no peas, no dessert. Aww.

Sam eats every pea. Does Sam get dessert? Yes! Promise one says so.

Sam hides the peas in a napkin. Does Sam get dessert? No! Promise two says so.

One sentence. **Both promises.** Grandma knew what she was doing.

<TryIt>
Make an "exactly when" rule with a grown-up. Then say promise one out loud. Then say promise two.
</TryIt>
