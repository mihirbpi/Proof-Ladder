You made a **promise**: *if it rains, I bring the umbrella.*

When did you break your promise?

Only when it rained and you had no umbrella.

If it rains and you have the umbrella, promise kept.

If it does not rain and you have the umbrella, promise kept.

If it does not rain and you don't have the umbrella, promise kept.

The only bad case is rain with no umbrella.
