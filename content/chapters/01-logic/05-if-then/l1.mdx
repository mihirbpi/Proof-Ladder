You tell your friend Sam:

> "If it rains, I will bring my umbrella."

That is a **promise**. Big words. Now you have to keep it.

When would you break your **promise**?

Only on a rainy day with no umbrella. Drip. Drip. Oops.

Here are the four ways your day can go.

<Figure alt="A 2 by 2 grid of pictures. Rain plus umbrella: promise kept. Rain plus no umbrella: promise broken. Sun plus umbrella: promise kept. Sun plus no umbrella: promise kept.">
  <UmbrellaCases />
</Figure>

Rain and umbrella? Dry! **Promise kept.**

Rain and no umbrella? Soggy! **Promise broken.** That is the bad one.

Sunny and you carried the umbrella anyway? **Promise kept.** Silly, but kept.

Sunny and no umbrella? **Promise kept.** Your promise was about rain.

Only one day can break it. Every other day, you win.

<TryIt>
Say the promise out loud. Then point to the one bad day. Then say the promise again, louder.
</TryIt>
