You tell your friend Sam:

> "If it rains, I will have my umbrella."

That is a **promise**.

When do you break your **promise**?

Only if it rains and you have no umbrella.

Look at the four ways your day can go.

<Figure alt="A 2 by 2 grid of pictures. Rain plus umbrella: promise kept. Rain plus no umbrella: promise broken. Sun plus umbrella: promise kept. Sun plus no umbrella: promise kept.">
  <UmbrellaCases />
</Figure>

Rain and umbrella — promise kept.

Rain and no umbrella — that is the bad one.

Sunny day with an umbrella — promise kept. Your **promise** was about rain, not sun.

Sunny day with no umbrella — promise kept. Same reason.

Only one bad day breaks your promise. All the other days, your promise stays kept.

<TryIt>
Say the **promise** out loud. Then point to the one bad day in the picture. Then say the promise again.
</TryIt>
