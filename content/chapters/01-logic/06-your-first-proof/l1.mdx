Sam builds a block tower. Ten blocks high. Wobble, wobble.

<Blocks colors={["red", "blue", "green", "yellow"]} label="wobble, wobble" />

You say: "That tower is going to fall."

Sam says: "How do you know?"

Now you have to show your thinking. Ready?

*I know the tower will fall **because** it is leaning. And things that lean fall over.*

That is how you know! You started from what you're given — the tower is leaning — and you said a **because**.

A **because** is the middle of your thinking, said out loud.

Crash! Sam's tower falls. You were right, and you said why *before* it happened.
