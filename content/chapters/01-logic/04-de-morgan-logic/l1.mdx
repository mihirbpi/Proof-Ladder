Sam is going outside. Sam needs a hat **and** a coat.

Now Sam runs out the door. Grandma shouts:

*"Sam! You do NOT have your hat and your coat!"*

Uh oh. What is missing?

Maybe the hat. Maybe the coat. Maybe both!

We only know one thing. Something is missing.

**Not both** does not mean *nothing*. It means *at least one is missing*.

Sam comes back wearing the coat. No hat. Grandma was right!

<TryIt>
Put on two things — say, one shoe and one sock. Now take off just one. Ask a grown-up: "Do I have both?" The answer is no. Ask them which one is missing.
</TryIt>
