Look at the door. Go on, look!

<Door both={true} />

*The door is open.*

Now say the **opposite**:

*The door is NOT open.*

Only one of those can be right.

Is the door open? Then the first one wins. The **opposite** loses.

Is the door shut? Then the **opposite** wins.

They can never both win. They can never both lose. One is true. The other is not.

Now here is a funny one. Biscuit the dog is asleep on the rug.

<Biscuit />

*Biscuit is asleep.* True.

*Biscuit is NOT asleep.* Not true. Shhh.

Point at three things in the room. Say a telling about each one. Then say the opposite in your biggest voice. Then say which one is true.
