Put two hoops on the floor. Let them overlap in the middle.

Left hoop: red things. Right hoop: round things.

Now here comes a red ball.

Red? Yes. Round? Yes. So it goes in the middle. The middle is for both.

Here comes a red sock. Red, but not round. Left hoop.

Here comes an orange. Round, but not red. Right hoop.

**And** means both. "Red **and** round" is only the ball.

**Or** means at least one. "Red **or** round" is the sock, the orange, *and* the ball too.

The ball does not have to pick. **Or** lets it stay.

Make two hoops with string or tape. Grab five things. Say "red or round" and put them where they go.
