Two kinds of claim. Two jobs each. That makes four.

**"Every block is red."**

To say YES: **take any one** block — you do not get to pick — and show it is red.

To say NO: find one bad apple. A green block. Done!

**"There is a red block."**

To say YES: dig one out and hold it up. Done!

To say NO: check every single block. Oof. That is the long one.

So sometimes finding one is the answer. Sometimes checking all is the answer.

The first word tells you which. **Every** or **there is**. Listen for it!

<TryIt>
Get a pile of things. Have a grown-up make a claim. Then say out loud: "Do I look for one, or do I check them all?"
</TryIt>
