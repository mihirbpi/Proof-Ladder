Some sentences tell you something.

*The cat has four legs.* That is true.

*The cat has nine legs.* That is silly.

Both of these are **tellings**. A **telling** is a sentence that could be true or could be silly.

Now try this one:

*Pick up the cat.*

That is not a **telling**. That is an asking. Askings are not tellings.

And this one:

*Is the cat sleepy?*

That is a question. Questions are not tellings either.
