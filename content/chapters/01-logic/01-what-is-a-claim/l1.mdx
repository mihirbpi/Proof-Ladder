This is Noodle. Noodle is a cat.

<Noodle numberLegs={true} />

*Noodle has four legs.*

True! Count them. One, two, three, four.

*Noodle has nine legs.*

Silly! Imagine the shoes.

Both of those are **tellings**. A **telling** is a sentence that could be true or could be silly.

Now try this one:

*Go pat Noodle.*

That is not a telling. That is an asking.

And this one:

*Is Noodle sleepy?*

That is a question. Questions are not tellings.

<Noodle mood="sleepy" />

Noodle is very sleepy. But the question did not tell us. It asked us.

<TryIt>
Say three tellings about a pet or a toy. Make one extra silly. Then sneak in one asking. See if a grown-up catches it.
</TryIt>
