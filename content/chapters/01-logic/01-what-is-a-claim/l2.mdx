A **telling** is a sentence that says something could be true or could be false. You can check it.

Here are some tellings:

- The cat has four legs.
- It is raining outside.
- Two plus three equals five.
- The apple is blue.

Some of these are true. Some are false. All of them are tellings, because you could figure out which one they are.

These are **not** tellings:

- Please pass the water.
- Is your name Sam?
- Wow!

The first one is asking you to do something. The second one is a question. The third one is just a shout. None of them can be true or false — so none of them are tellings.

## Some tellings you can check right now

If I say "the sky is blue," you can look up and check. But if I say "there is a lost penny under Sam's bed," you would have to go look. Both are still tellings — it's just that some are easy to check and some are hard.

There are even tellings that no grown-up on Earth has figured out yet. That doesn't stop them from being tellings.
