Here is your promise again:

> "If it rains, I will bring my umbrella."

Now let's **flip it around**. Say it backwards:

> "If I have my umbrella, it is raining."

Wait. Is that the same promise?

No! Look outside. Sunshine. And there you are, holding the umbrella.

Maybe you like the umbrella. Maybe it is your favorite. Maybe you forgot to put it away!

You did not break any promise. You just have an umbrella on a sunny day.

So when you **flip it around**, you get a different promise. Not the same one.

<TryIt>
Say: *If I am asleep, my eyes are shut.* Now flip it around: *If my eyes are shut, I am asleep.* Shut your eyes and stay wide awake. You just broke the flipped one!
</TryIt>
