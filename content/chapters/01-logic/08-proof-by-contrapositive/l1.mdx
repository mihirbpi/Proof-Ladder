Sam makes a promise:

> "If I go outside, I wear my boots."

How do you check it? You could follow Sam around all day. Boring!

Try a **backwards check** instead. Look at Sam's feet.

No boots? Then Sam did not go outside. Not once!

That is the same promise, checked from the other end. It is much faster.

You did not follow Sam anywhere. You just looked at the feet.

Sometimes the backwards way is the easy way.

<TryIt>
Look for someone's shoes by the door. If the shoes are there, is the person outside? Say your answer, and say your because.
