Let's play the **why game**. I say something. You ask why. Ready?

*The cup fell down.*

Why?

*Because you pushed it off the table.*

Why did it fall when I pushed it?

*Because things that leave the table go down.*

Why do things go down?

*Because... they just do. Everything falls. **We just agree** on that.*

There! We reached the bottom.

Every why has an answer, and every answer has another why underneath. But it cannot go on forever. Somewhere you reach something you both just agree on.

Those are **the rules we start with**. Math has them too.
