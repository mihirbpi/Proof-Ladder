Tape a number line on the floor. $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, and more.

Stand on $3$.

Now we will do $3 + 2$. Ready?

**Press** the next button. Step forward. You are on $4$.

**Press** again. Step forward. You are on $5$.

That is two presses. Stop!

You landed on $5$. And $3 + 2 = 5$.

Adding is just pressing next, over and over. The second number tells you how many presses.

Try $4 + 3$. Stand on $4$. Press, press, press. Where are you?

$7$!
