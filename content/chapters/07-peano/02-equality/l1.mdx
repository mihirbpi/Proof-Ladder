Let's play the **same as** game. Three rules. Act them all out!

**Rule one.** Hold up your red block. Is it the same as itself?

Of course! Everything is the same as itself. That one is easy.

**Rule two.** Your block is the same color as Sam's block.

So is Sam's block the same color as yours?

Yes! It works both ways. It always does.

**Rule three.** Your block matches Sam's. And Sam's block matches Pip's.

Does yours match Pip's?

Yes! Same as passes along the line.

Three rules. And **same as** always follows all three.

<TryIt>
Find three things that are the same color. Do rule one, then rule two, then rule three, out loud.
