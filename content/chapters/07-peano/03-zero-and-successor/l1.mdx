Here is a machine with one big button on it.

You put a number in. You press the button. Out comes the **next** number.

Let's start at $0$.

Press! Out comes $1$.

Put the $1$ back in. Press! Out comes $2$.

Press again. $3$!

Press again. $4$!

<SuccessorChain mode="chain" count={5} />

You could press that button all day. You could press it for a hundred years.

There is always a next one. The machine never gets stuck and never runs out.

And that is all counting is. Start at zero, and keep pressing.

<TryIt>
Make a button with your fist. Start at 0 and press it ten times, saying the next number each time. Could you keep going?
</TryIt>
