Get some counters. We are going to make **groups of** two.

Here is one group of two. Count them: $1$, $2$.

Here is another group of two. Now count them all: $1, 2, 3, 4$.

One more group of two! Count them all again: $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6$.

Three groups of two makes **six**!

Now look at what you actually did. You took two, then added two more, then added two more.

$2$, and $2$ more, and $2$ more. That is $6$.

So making equal groups is just adding the same thing again and again.

And what about **zero** groups of two? You have nothing at all! Zero.
