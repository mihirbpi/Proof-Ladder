Look at the counting line. $0$, then $1$, then $2$, then $3$, going on forever.

<SuccessorChain mode="chain" count={4} />

Now imagine two strange creatures sneak in from the side. Call them Blip and Blop.

Blip points at Blop. Blop points back at Blip.

They point at each other forever, round and round. And they are not on the counting line at all — you can never reach them by counting from zero.

<SuccessorChain mode="rogue" count={4} rogueLabels={["Blip", "Blop"]} />

Are Blip and Blop numbers?

**No!** Counting numbers are the ones you reach by starting at zero and pressing next.

So here is the last rule: **no extras**. Nothing gets in unless you can count your way to it.

Sorry, Blip. Sorry, Blop. Out you go.

<TryIt>
Count from 0 to 10 out loud. Could you reach Blip that way? No! That is why Blip is not a number.
</TryIt>
