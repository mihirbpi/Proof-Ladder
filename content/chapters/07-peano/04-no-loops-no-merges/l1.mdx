Let's try a **broken machine**.

Start at $0$. Press! Out comes $1$. Press! Out comes... $0$ again!

$0$, $1$, $0$, $1$, $0$, $1$. Round and round forever.

<SuccessorChain mode="loop" count={4} />

Is that counting? No! Real counting never comes home. You never get back to zero.

So here is a rule: **nothing is ever the next one after something and also the start.**

Now here is another broken machine.

Press on $2$ — out comes $5$. Press on $3$ — out comes $5$ too!

Two different numbers, and they share the same next one. Squished together!

<SuccessorChain mode="merge" count={4} />

Real counting never squishes. So here is another rule: **two different numbers never share a next one.**

Two rules. Two broken machines fixed.

Count out loud: 0, 1, 0, 1, 0, 1. Does that feel like counting? Now count properly and see the difference.
