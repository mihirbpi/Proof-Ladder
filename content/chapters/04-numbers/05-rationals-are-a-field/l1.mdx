One cookie. Two friends. Share it!

Snap. Now each friend has **a piece**.

How much is a piece? Not a whole cookie. Not nothing. Something in between.

<ThingRow items={[{kind:"half-cookie"},{kind:"half-cookie"}]}
  groups={[1, 1]} groupLabels={["one friend", "the other"]}
  caption="one cookie, snapped into two halves" />

We call it a *half*.

Is a half a real amount? Absolutely! You can hold it. You can eat it. It is quite tasty.

Now snap a cookie into four. Each **piece** is a quarter. Also a real amount.

Two of those quarters, put together, make a half again.

So pieces are numbers too. They just are not whole ones.

And here is the good news — you can never break a piece so small that it stops being a number.

<TryIt>
Fold a piece of paper in half. Now in half again. And again! Each part is still a real amount, just smaller.
