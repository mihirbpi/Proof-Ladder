Draw a square. A nice fair one, all sides the same.

Now draw **the slanted line** from one corner to the opposite corner.

Look at it. It is a real line! You drew it. You can put your finger on it.

Now try to measure it.

Is it $1$? No, it is longer than a side.

Is it $1$ and a half? No, a little shorter than that.

Is it $1$ and a bit? Keep trying halves, quarters, eighths...

You will never land on it exactly. Not with any piece. Ever.

Grown-ups can even *prove* you never will. That line has a length no fraction can catch.
