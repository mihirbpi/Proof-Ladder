Get 6 counters. Now pair them up. Two, two, two.

All paired! Nothing left. That is a fair share.

Now get 7 counters. Pair them up again. Two, two, two...

**One left over!** That one has no partner. Poor thing.

Try 8. All paired. Try 9. **One left over.**

Try 10. All paired! Try 11. **One left over.**

Do you see it? It goes: paired, one over, paired, one over. Back and forth, forever.

And it is never *two* left over. Two left over would just be one more pair!
