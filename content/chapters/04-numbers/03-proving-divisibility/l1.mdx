Get 12 counters. Share them into 2 big piles.

6 in each pile. Nothing left over!

Now take one big pile of 6 and share *that* into 3 little piles.

2 in each. Nothing left over again!

Do the same to the other big pile. Now count your little piles.

3 little piles, and 3 more little piles. That is 6 little piles!

And every little pile has 2 counters. Nothing left over anywhere.

So you shared 12 into 6 groups — and you never even tried to.

Sharing twice does the job all by itself.
