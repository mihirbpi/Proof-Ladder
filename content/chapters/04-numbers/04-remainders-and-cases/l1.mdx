Three friends. Let's share some counters.

$6$ counters: two each. Nothing **left over**!

$7$ counters: two each, and **one left over**.

$8$ counters: two each, and **two left over**.

<ShareOut total={8} groups={3} mode="plates" caption="eight counters, three friends: two each and two left over" />

$9$ counters: three each. Nothing left over again!

Now watch. Could you ever have **three** left over?

No! Three left over is another one for each friend. So it would not be left over at all.

So sharing among three friends, you get $0$, $1$, or $2$ left over. Only those.

Three friends, three kinds of leftover. Neat!
