You have 3 cookies. Sam brings 4 more.

Now you have 7 cookies. Still **whole** cookies! Nothing got squished.

Try again. 2 cookies, then 5 more. That is 7. Whole again.

Adding never breaks a cookie. You always end up with whole ones.

Now try sharing.

7 cookies. Two people. Share them fairly.

3 each... and one left over. Uh oh.

Snap! You have to break the last cookie in half.

<ThingRow items={[{kind:"cookie"},{kind:"cookie"},{kind:"cookie"},{kind:"half-cookie"},{kind:"cookie"},{kind:"cookie"},{kind:"cookie"},{kind:"half-cookie"}]}
  groups={[4, 4]} groupLabels={["one person", "the other"]} caption="sharing snapped a cookie" />

So sharing *can* break a cookie. Adding never can.

That is a real difference, and grown-up mathematicians care about it a lot.
