Remember the slanted line across the square? Its length is a **strange number**. No fraction fits it.

Now here is a puzzle.

Take that strange length. Now take the very same length again, but going backwards.

Put them together. What do you get?

**Nothing!** You go forward and then straight back. You are exactly where you started. Zero.

And zero is not strange at all. Zero is the most ordinary number there is.

So two strange numbers added up to a perfectly ordinary one.

Strange plus strange does not have to stay strange. Sneaky!
