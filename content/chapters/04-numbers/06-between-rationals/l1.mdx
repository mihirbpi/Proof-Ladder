Here is a ruler. Point at $2$. Now point at $3$.

Is there a spot **in between**? Yes! Two and a half. Right there.

Now point at $2$ and at two and a half. Anything in between those?

Yes! Two and a quarter. It fits.

Now point at $2$ and two and a quarter. In between?

Yes again! Smaller and smaller, but always something there.

Could you ever find two spots so close together that nothing fits between them?

Never! There is always room. Always.

<TryIt>
Hold up two fingers a little apart. Now put a third finger between them. Now try to squeeze in a fourth. When do you run out? (Your fingers run out. The ruler never does.)
