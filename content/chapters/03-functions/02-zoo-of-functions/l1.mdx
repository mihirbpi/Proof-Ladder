Machines come in all kinds. Here are three.

**The first-letter machine.** Put in a name, out comes its first letter.

Sam goes in. Out comes S. Pip goes in. Out comes P.

**The cubby machine.** Put in a child, out comes their cubby. You know this one.

**The color machine.** Put in a toy, out comes its color.

Red ball goes in. Out comes red. Blue truck goes in. Out comes blue.

Look — no numbers anywhere! A **machine** does not need numbers.

It just needs to give every thing exactly one answer.
