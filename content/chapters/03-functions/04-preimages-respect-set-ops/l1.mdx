Point at cubby 1. **Trace back.** Who put a coat in there? Sam did.

Now point at cubby 2. Trace back. Ada and Bo!

Now do something new. Point at cubby 1 **and** cubby 2, both at once.

Trace back from both. Who do you get?

<Cubbies cubbies={["1", "2", "3"]}
  hung={[{child: "Sam", cubby: 0}, {child: "Ada", cubby: 1}, {child: "Pip", cubby: 1}]}
  caption="trace back from cubby 1 and cubby 2" />

Sam, Ada, and Bo. All three!

Look — that is just the first group and the second group put together.

So tracing back from two cubbies is the same as tracing back from each one and joining the answers.

You could do it either way. You get the same children.
