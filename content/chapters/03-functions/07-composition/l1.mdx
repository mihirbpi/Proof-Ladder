Here is machine one: put in a child, out comes their cubby.

Here is machine two: put in a cubby, out comes its color.

Now line them up. Put them **in a row**.

Sam goes in the first machine. Out comes cubby 3.

Take cubby 3 and put it in the second machine. Out comes **green**.

So Sam goes all the way through and comes out green!

Two machines **in a row** make one big machine: child in, color out.

But be careful. The second machine only takes cubbies. If you tried to put a *color* in, it would not know what to do.
