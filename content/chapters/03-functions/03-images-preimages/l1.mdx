**Question one: which cubbies got used?**

Cubby 1 — a coat! **Used.**

Cubby 2 — a coat! **Used.**

Cubby 3 — empty. Nobody went there.


So only some cubbies got used. That is fine. A machine does not have to fill every cubby.

**Question two: who is in there?**

Point at cubby 2. Who put a coat in it? Sam did! And Pip did too — they share.

Now point at cubby 3. **Who is in there?**

Nobody. Not one child. And that is a real answer.

<TryIt>
Put toys into cups, but leave one cup empty. Now say which cups got used. Then pick a cup and say who is in there.
