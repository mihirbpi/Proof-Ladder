Look at the cubbies again. Here is a brand new question.

**Does anybody have to share?**

In some classrooms, Sam and Pip squeeze into cubby 2 together. Two coats, one hook. **Sharing!**

In other classrooms, everybody gets their very own cubby. Nobody shares at all.

That second kind is extra tidy. It has a special feeling: if you find a coat, you know exactly whose it is.

With sharing, you cannot tell. Is that Sam's coat or Pip's? Who knows!

So: **no sharing** means you can always trace a coat back to one child.

<TryIt>
Put toys in cups. First squeeze two toys into one cup. Then try again with a cup for every toy. Which one lets you say whose is whose?
