Now we want both tidy things at once.

**Nobody shares.** Every child has their own cubby.

**Nothing left out.** Every cubby has a coat in it.

When both are true, the machine is **just right**.

And here comes the amazing part. Ready?

You do not have to count!

Look — one child, one cubby. Another child, another cubby. On and on, and at the end there is nothing left over on either side.

So there are just as many children as cubbies. You never counted a single one.

You just paired them up.

<TryIt>
Get a pile of spoons and a pile of cups. Put one spoon in each cup. If nothing is left over, there are the same number — and you never counted!
</TryIt>
