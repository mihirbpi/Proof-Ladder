Home time! Everybody grab your coat.

Point at a cubby. Whose coat is that? **Undo** the machine and find out.

Cubby 1 — Sam's. Cubby 2 — Pip's. Cubby 3 — Ada's.

Easy! Everyone gets the right coat.

Now imagine a messy day. Sam and Pip shared cubby 2.

Point at cubby 2. Whose coat? Sam's or Pip's? **You cannot tell!** No undo.

Or imagine cubby 3 was empty all day. Point at it. Whose coat? **Nobody's!** Nothing to undo.

So you can only **undo** when nothing got shared and nothing got left out.

Just right, both ways. Then everybody goes home in their own coat.
