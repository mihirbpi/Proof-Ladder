Last time we asked: does anybody share?

Here is a different question. Look at the wall of cubbies again.

**Did every cubby get used?**

Cubby 1 — a coat. Used!

Cubby 2 — a coat. Used!

Cubby 3 — empty. Nobody went there.

So no. Not **all used up**.

Now imagine a classroom where every single cubby has a coat in it. Not one empty hook.

That is a full machine. **All used up!**

Two different tidy things. One is about sharing. This one is about leftovers.

<TryIt>
Put toys in cups until every cup has something. Then add one more empty cup and look at it. Is it all used up now?
