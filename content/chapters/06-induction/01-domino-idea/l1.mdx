Stand up a long line of **dominoes**. Or blocks. Or books!

Push the first one. *Click, click, click...* They all fall down!

Why did they all fall? Two things had to be true.

**One:** somebody pushed the first one.

**Two:** each one was close enough to knock over the next.

Now break it. Move one domino far away from the rest.

Push the first one again. *Click, click...* stop!

The line stopped at the gap. Everything after it is still standing.

Now put them all back close, but do not push. Nothing happens at all.

So you need **both**. Push the first, and let each one reach the next.
