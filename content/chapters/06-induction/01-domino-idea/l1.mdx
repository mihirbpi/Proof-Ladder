Stand up a long line of **dominoes**. Or blocks. Or books!

Push the first one. *Click, click, click...* They all fall down!

<Figure alt="A row of dominoes standing on a line. The first few have fallen, each tipping into the next, and the rest are still standing waiting their turn.">
  <DominoRow count={6} fallen={3} pushed={true} trailing={false} />
</Figure>

Why did they all fall? Two things had to be true.

**One:** somebody pushed the first one.

**Two:** each one was close enough to knock over the next.

Now break it. Move one domino far away from the rest.

Push the first one again. *Click, click...* stop!

The line stopped at the gap. Everything after it is still standing.

<Figure alt="The same row, but with a wide space after the third domino. The first three have fallen and the rest stand untouched, because the falling could not cross the gap.">
  <DominoRow count={6} fallen={3} gapAfter={2} pushed={true} trailing={false} />
</Figure>

Now put them all back close, but do not push. Nothing happens at all.

So you need **both**. Push the first, and let each one reach the next.

<TryIt>
Line up 6 blocks. Push the first. Now set them up again with a big gap in the middle and push again. Where does it stop?
</TryIt>
