Here is a pile of $3$ counters. Share them among $3$ friends. One each! Fair.

Now a pile of $15$. Share among $3$. Five each! Fair again.

Now $63$. Would that share fairly among $3$?

That is a big pile. You do not want to count it out.

But here is the thing: dominoes work for sharing questions too!

If you can show the first pile shares fairly, and you can show that each pile being fair makes the *next* pile fair, then **every** pile is fair.

You never have to count the big ones. The chain does it for you.

(It does share fairly, by the way. $63$ is $21$ each.)
