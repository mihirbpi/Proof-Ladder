To knock down a whole line of dominoes you have **two jobs**.

**Job one: push the first one.**

If nobody pushes, nothing happens. The dominoes just stand there all day.

**Job two: check each one touches the next.**

Walk along the line. Is this one close enough to that one? And that one to the next?

If yes all the way along, you are ready.

Do both jobs and the whole line falls. Every single domino, even the ones far away that you never touched.

Do only one job and it does not work. Try it and see!

<TryIt>
Set up 8 blocks. Do job two: check every gap is small. Then do job one: push! Now set them up again, leave one big gap, and see which job you skipped.
