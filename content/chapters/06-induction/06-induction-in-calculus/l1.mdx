Start with $1$ counter.

**Double** it. Now you have $2$.

Double again. $4$.

Double again. $8$.

Again! $16$.

Again! $32$.

Wow, that got big fast. And you did the same little thing every time — just double.

Now guess. What comes after $32$?

$64$! You knew, because the rule never changes.

And the rule will still be the same on the hundredth doubling. That is why you can guess so far ahead.
