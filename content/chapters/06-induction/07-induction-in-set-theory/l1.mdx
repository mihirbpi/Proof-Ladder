You have one toy: a bear. You are packing a **bag**.

How many different bags could you pack?

Bag one: the bear goes in.

Bag two: nothing goes in! An empty bag is still a bag you packed.

So **two** bags.

Now add a truck. Two toys. How many bags now?

Nothing. Just the bear. Just the truck. Both together.

That is **four** bags!

Now add a ball. Three toys. Count them with a grown-up.

**Eight** bags!

Two, four, eight. It doubles every time you add a toy!

<TryIt>
Get 3 small toys. Make every different bag you can — do not forget the empty one. Count them. Did you find 8?
</TryIt>
